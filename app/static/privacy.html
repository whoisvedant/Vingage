Privacy by design

Built to forget.

VINGAGE measures the mood of a room, not the people in it. Privacy is enforced by how the system is built, not by a policy page.

In memory, briefly

Per-person signals

  • Face landmarks and head pose
  • Eye openness, gaze, blinks, motion
  • A rolling 10-second window per face

Scored, then overwritten. A face that leaves the view is dropped after 3 seconds.

Never stored or sent

Anything identifying

  • Camera frames or video
  • Face images or landmark data
  • Names, IDs, face embeddings
  • Any per-person history or identity

There is no recording path in the code. The live view is shown only on this computer, can blur faces, and is never saved or sent over the network.

Shown and saved

Group statistics

  • Share of the audience facing the focus
  • Engagement-model share and mean score
  • Head count, for the minimum rule

Saved sessions hold only these numbers and a timestamp.

The minimum-group rule

No statistic is shown unless at least k people are counted. Try it.

Responsible use
  • Tell the people in the room that a camera is measuring group engagement.
  • Engagement here is an estimate from visible behaviour. A person looking away may be thinking; a person looking ahead may be daydreaming.
  • Do not use the output to rank, grade or discipline individuals. The system cannot produce individual results.
  • The training data (DAiSEE) was collected with participant consent for research and is not included in this project.
