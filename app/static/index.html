What it does

A pulse, not a surveillance feed.

01 / WATCH

A camera, pointed at the room

A laptop webcam, a phone or a CCTV feed. Each face is read for where its head points, in memory, and forgotten.

02 / ESTIMATE

A model scores each moment

Each head is compared with the direction of the presenter or screen. A model trained on 8,571 labelled clips adds a behavioural engagement score.

03 / SUMMARISE

Faces melt into one figure

Scores merge into a single group number. If fewer than three people are in view, the screen shows nothing at all.
