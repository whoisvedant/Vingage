About VINGAGE

Real-Time Audience Engagement Detection System: an Image and Video Processing project for CSET344 at Bennett University.

What it does

VINGAGE watches a room through an ordinary camera and estimates how engaged the audience is, as one group figure that updates every second. Presenters, teachers and event teams can see when attention rises or drops without filming or identifying anyone.

The system is general purpose: a lecture, a workshop, a meeting or a talk. It reports trends for the group only, and hides every figure when fewer than three people are in view.

Technology

VisionMediaPipe Face Landmarker, OpenCV, CLAHE, optical flow
ModelsLogistic regression, XGBoost, GRU, SHAP
Training dataDAiSEE (8,571 labelled clips)
ServerFastAPI, WebSocket
InterfaceHTML, CSS, JavaScript (no external services)
