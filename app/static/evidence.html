Two signals, kept separate

Attention on the focus

The headline number. Each tracked head is compared with the direction of the presenter or screen: set from the camera direction or by a 3-second calibration. It is plain geometry, instant, and needs no training. Not yet validated: it has not been measured against hand-labelled footage.

Engagement model

A logistic regression trained on DAiSEE (single learners at a laptop). It scores behaviour over 10 seconds: blinks, eye closure, movement, head pose. It is the benchmarked, trained part of the project, with the results below.

Results on the DAiSEE test split

What drives predictions · SHAP on XGBoost

Mean absolute SHAP value of the 15 most influential features, four-level task. Eye signals lead: variation in left blink, the share of closed-eye frames (PERCLOS) and right-eye inward look. This shows what the model uses, not what causes engagement.

Limits, stated plainly
  • Modest accuracy. Four-level macro-F1 is 0.33 at best. DAiSEE labels are noisy and badly unbalanced.
  • Rare classes. Level 0 is 0.2% of the test set, about 4 clips, so its recall means little.
  • Single run. One seed, default settings, no significance test. Small gaps are not wins.
  • Domain gap. Trained on one person facing a laptop. A classroom camera showing many people at an angle is not yet validated.
  • Group level only. The product reports audience trends, never judgements about a person.
